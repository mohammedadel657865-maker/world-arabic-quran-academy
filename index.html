Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know Arabic before I start?

No. Beginner learning paths can start with the Arabic alphabet, sounds and basic reading skills.

Can I study Arabic and the Quran together?

Yes. You can request a combined learning plan based on your goals and available study time.

Are lessons live or recorded?

The academy is designed to offer both live teacher-led lessons and recorded learning materials.

Can learners from any country join?

The online format is intended for learners around the world. Lesson times and payment options will need to be confirmed when the academy begins enrolment.
